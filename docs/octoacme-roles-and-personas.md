# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

Project needs may be met by dedicated people or by team members taking on more than one role. Assignments should be made explicit for each project.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers understand user needs and shape accessible, usable experiences that support the product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research and usability evaluations
- Create and maintain user flows, interaction designs, and interface specifications
- Incorporate accessibility and usability considerations into designs
- Validate design decisions with users and communicate findings

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, understandable, and accessible
- Reduce usability risks before and during implementation

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews with the Product Manager and Stakeholders
- Design specifications and implementation discussions with Developers
- Usability and accessibility review with QA/Testing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs and research findings to product priorities and acceptance criteria.
- Collaborate with Developers and the Technical Lead to assess feasibility and resolve implementation questions.
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility in validation.
- Keep the Project Manager informed of research dependencies, design decisions, and risks; share relevant findings with Stakeholders.

---

## Technical Leads or Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound, maintainable implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review architecture and technical approaches
- Establish engineering practices and quality expectations
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support Developers with design decisions and complex implementation work

### Goals
- Deliver secure, reliable, maintainable solutions
- Resolve technical uncertainty and dependencies early

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and decisions with Developers and the Product Manager
- Dependency and risk updates with the Project Manager
- Implementation and testability discussions with QA/Testing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advise the Product Manager on technical options and trade-offs without taking over product prioritization.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and technical risks that affect delivery plans.
- Guide Developers through architecture and engineering decisions, and work with QA/Testing on quality and testability.
- Consult Stakeholders when technical decisions affect their systems or commitments.

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Coordinate risk reviews and recommend mitigations
- Advise on secure design, data handling, and access controls
- Track findings through verification and escalation

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risks before release
- Make security and privacy ownership and outstanding decisions visible

### Typical Communication
- Risk and mitigation reviews with the Technical Lead and Project Manager
- Requirements and trade-offs with the Product Manager
- Control implementation and verification with Developers and QA/Testing
- Escalations through the appropriate security or privacy channels

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to identify security and privacy requirements that affect scope and acceptance criteria.
- Partner with the Technical Lead and Developers to incorporate mitigations into the design and implementation.
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security-related validation and evidence.
- Keep the Project Manager informed of risks, owners, and escalation needs; involve Stakeholders when their data, systems, or obligations are affected.

---

## Release and DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Engineers enable reliable delivery by managing deployment automation, environments, and operational visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or improve build, test, and deployment automation
- Prepare and manage environments needed for delivery
- Define monitoring, alerting, and operational verification for releases
- Plan rollback or mitigation steps with the delivery team

### Goals
- Make deployments repeatable, observable, and low risk
- Ensure teams can detect and respond to release issues

### Typical Communication
- Environment and pipeline coordination with Developers and the Technical Lead
- Release readiness and smoke-test planning with QA/Testing
- Deployment timing and status with the Project Manager and Product Manager

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers and the Technical Lead on pipeline changes, environment needs, and deployment design.
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure release checks and smoke tests are supported by the deployment process.
- Provide the Project Manager with readiness, risks, and rollback information for release planning and communication.
- Align with the Product Manager on release timing and operational impact, and share relevant deployment information with Stakeholders.

---

## Customer Support or Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and service-operational needs into project planning and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Identify customer support needs and operational impacts of proposed changes
- Prepare support teams with release information, known issues, and guidance
- Relay recurring customer feedback and operational issues to the delivery team
- Participate in incident coordination and post-release follow-up as appropriate

### Goals
- Help customers and service teams transition smoothly after changes
- Ensure operational feedback informs product improvements

### Typical Communication
- Customer-impact and prioritization discussions with the Product Manager
- Readiness and escalation updates with the Project Manager
- Known issues, troubleshooting, and incident information with Developers and QA/Testing
- Release briefings and support materials with Stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to surface customer needs, feedback, and support impacts for backlog decisions.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on readiness tasks, communications, and operational dependencies.
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify expected behavior, known limitations, and investigation steps.
- Share release and incident information with Stakeholders and route relevant findings back to the product and delivery team.
